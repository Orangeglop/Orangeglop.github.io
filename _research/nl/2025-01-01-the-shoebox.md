I always have a box with me since young, not ONE box, but A box, where I store all random things from events with friends, small gifts, notes, sketches, postcards, etc.

It comes to the point of a lot of the items are bottle caps, candy wrappers, tea boxes, etc.

Why?
To me, each of them held a very specific memory
Must there are others with the same “fetish” as me.
I asked my friends, surprisingly, a lot of them have something similar: some are proper treasure box, some have not only one, but a few at the same time
